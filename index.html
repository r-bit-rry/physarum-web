The brush circle's colour shows the current action: cyan attract, orange repel, green paint, grey erase, white walls (dashed = erase), magenta spawn.

About

A real-time Physarum (slime mould) simulation running on the GPU in your browser: millions of agents that sense, follow and deposit trails.

Web version

Browser port (WebGPU / WebGL2), interaction and development by Roy Belio (r-bit-rry).

Original simulation

Created by Sebastian Lague in Unity: SebLague/Slime-Simulation and the video Coding Adventure: Ant and Slime Simulations. The presets and the core algorithm come from his project.

Research

Based on Jeff Jones, Characteristics of pattern formation and evolution in approximations of Physarum transport networks, Artificial Life 16(2), 2010 (doi).

His wider work on multi-agent Physarum models is collected in the book From Pattern Formation to Material Computation: Multi-agent Modelling of Physarum Polycephalum (Springer, 2015).
